## 2. Dignity Through Autonomy
Traditional responses often advise senior citizens to avoid digital payments or rely entirely on children and neighbors. Sakha restores dignity and autonomy by:
1. **Providing an On-Device Safety Cushion:** Seniors can browse and transact with peace of mind knowing accidental clicks on malicious lookalikes are blocked before credentials can be entered.
2. **Preventing Visual Exploits:** By automatically blurring Aadhaar, PAN, and card numbers with a tap-to-reveal toggle, shoulder surfing and screen recording malware are neutralized.
3. **Plain Regional Language Explanations:** Explaining page demands in Telugu and English with zero security jargon respects the user's native tongue and lived experience.

## 3. Measurable Societal Outcomes
- **Financial Loss Prevention:** Blocking credential harvesting combinations prevents irreversible unauthorized debits.
- **Cognitive Load Reduction:** Seniors no longer need to scrutinize subtle differences in character encoding (homoglyphs) or cryptic SSL certificates.
